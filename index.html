In development

Build the maze that holds them longest.

Maze Rivals is a quick two-player duel for your phone. You and your rival get the same board. Each of you builds a maze with walls and traps, and the maze that slows the runners longest wins the round.

Coming to Android and iPhone. It is not available to download yet.

How a match works

  1. The same board for both of you. Every round, you and your rival get the very same board, with a start gate and an exit gate.
  2. Build your maze. Spend your budget on walls, frost towers, tar and snare glyphs to slow the runners down.
  3. Reveal and replay. Your mazes stay hidden until you have both handed them in. Then watch both runs and see whose maze held the runners longer.

Made for real life

Take your turn when you like

You do not need to be online at the same time. Friendly matches give you up to 72 hours for each turn. A match is three rounds.

Fair by design

The server checks and scores every run, and your phone can replay it to confirm. Playing is never pay-to-win: purchases, when they arrive, are cosmetic only.
