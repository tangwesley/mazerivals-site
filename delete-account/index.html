Delete your Maze Rivals account

Pre-release draft. Maze Rivals is not released yet. This page describes how the game is meant to work when it is released, and it will be finalised before then.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Maze Rivals account and the information tied to it.

What is deleted

  • Your display name, avatar, rating and league.
  • Your friend code, friends, friend requests and blocks.
  • Your notification settings and the push token of your phone.
  • Your sign-in: the account ID can no longer be used.

What stays, and for how long

  • Matches also belong to your opponent's history. After deletion they show you as "Deleted player", without your name or avatar.
  • Deleted information can remain in our backups for up to 7 days, then it expires.
  • A short record of reports and safety actions may be kept for as long as the law or player safety requires.
  • Technical request records are kept for 30 days, as described in the privacy policy.

How to delete

Without the game. Write to support@firebirdstudio.games with the subject "Delete my Maze Rivals account" and include your friend code (it looks like ABCD-EFGH and is shown on the Friends screen). To make sure only the owner can delete an account, we will reply with a short word and ask you to set it as your display name in Settings > Profile; once we see it we delete the account. If you can no longer open the game, tell us and we will explain what we can do. We answer within 30 days and tell you when the deletion is done.

Deleting an account cannot be undone. Just uninstalling the game does not delete your account.
