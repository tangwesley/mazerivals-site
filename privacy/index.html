Privacy Policy - Maze Rivals

Pre-release draft. Maze Rivals is not released yet. This page describes how the game is meant to work when it is released, and it will be finalised before then.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Wesley Tang, trading as Firebird Studio ("we", "us") makes Maze Rivals. This page explains what the game collects, why, who can see it, and how you can have it deleted. Questions: support@firebirdstudio.games.

Who can play

Maze Rivals is for people aged 13 and over. It is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to support@firebirdstudio.games and we will delete it.

What we collect, and why

Account ID. When you first open the game, Google's Firebase Authentication creates a random account ID for you. You do not need an email address, password or phone number. We use the ID to know which account is yours and to keep it safe.

Profile. A display name (the game gives you one and you may change it), an avatar choice, whether other players can find you by friend search, and your rating and league. Other players you meet in the game can see your display name, avatar and rating.

Friends. Your friend code, the friends you add, friend requests, and the players you block. We use these so you can play with friends and so blocked players cannot contact you.

Game data. Your matches, the mazes you build and where you place pieces, scores, round results and replays. We use them to run the game and work out who wins. Your maze stays hidden from your opponent until both of you have submitted the round; after that, your opponent can watch a replay that shows both mazes.

Notifications (only if you allow them). If you allow notifications on your phone, we store a push token from your phone's notification service, the phone type (Android or iPhone), your language and your time zone, and your alert settings (for example quiet hours). We use them to tell you when it is your turn or a round is decided. You can switch notifications off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Technical records. Our servers keep standard records of each request: the time, your IP address, the game version, and any error. We use them to keep the game secure and to fix problems. They are kept for 30 days.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your contacts, photos, microphone or location. We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number or payment details. The game currently has no ads, no purchases and no analytics or crash-reporting tools. If that changes, this page will change first and the game will ask for any choice the law requires.

This website

This website (https://mazerivals.app) is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add cookies, analytics or advertising to it. GitHub, as the host, may process visitors' IP addresses and technical details under its own privacy statement.

Who we share it with

We use Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Run and Firebase Cloud Messaging) to run the game, and Apple's notification service to reach iPhones. They process information for us under their own terms. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We may share information if the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

Where it is kept

Our servers are in the United States. If you play from another country, your information is sent there.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we remove your profile, friends, blocks and notification details. Matches also belong to your opponent's history, so after deletion they show you as "Deleted player" without your name or avatar. Deleted information can remain in our backups for up to 7 days before it expires. We may keep a short record of reports and safety actions for as long as the law or player safety requires.

Your choices

  • Change your display name or avatar, or hide yourself from friend search, in Settings > Profile.
  • Turn off notifications in Settings > Alerts or in your phone's settings.
  • Block a player from the Friends screen.
  • Delete your account: see https://mazerivals.app/delete-account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, by writing to support@firebirdstudio.games. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, such as objecting to certain uses; write to us and we will answer within the time the law requires.

Security

The game talks to our servers over encrypted connections, and our servers check every action instead of trusting the phone. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here with a new date, and we will tell you in the game if the change is important.
